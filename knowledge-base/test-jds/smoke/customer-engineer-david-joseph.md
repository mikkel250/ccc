About the Company
Our client is building the gateway to the internet for AI agents. As information consumption shifts from humans searching pre-crawled indexes to AI agents performing real-time, targeted crawling from sources of truth, the client builds the APIs AI agents use to access that data — live company and people data via APIs and full dataset delivery, piping live data from over a dozen sources and delivering it instantly at scale. As much a builder's seat as a support seat.
